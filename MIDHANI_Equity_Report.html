Company Overview

What They Do

Mishra Dhatu Nigam Limited (MIDHANI), Hyderabad, makes titanium alloys, superalloys and special steels for defence, space and nuclear/energy customers. It is a Ministry of Defence PSU, a Miniratna (category I/II not found), and the President of India (Government of India) holds 74.00%, unchanged through the last four quarters. An earlier DIPAM plan to sell 10% by offer for sale (2019–20) was never completed.

FY26 sales were ₹1,209 Cr, operating profit ₹239 Cr (19.8% by my arithmetic) and net profit ₹131 Cr (Screener, standalone; consolidated is nearly equal; multibagg.ai: turnover ₹1,208.63 Cr, PAT ₹130.79 Cr, +18.8%). TTM to Jun-26 sales are ₹1,278 Cr. Revenue is heavily Q4-weighted: Mar-26 alone was ₹553 Cr, 46% of FY26 (my arithmetic). Q1 FY27: revenue from operations ₹239.49 Cr (+40.46%), PAT ₹16.31 Cr (+27.42%). Titanium output was ~700 t in FY26, nearly double the prior year (multibagg.ai, secondary), and a 500 tpa vacuum arc remelting (VAR) facility is commissioned and ramping (secondary).

Key Competitive Advantage: Decades of alloy and process qualification at the customers that matter (HAL, DRDO, ISRO, nuclear and defence programmes), backed by Government of India ownership and a strategic-importance rating basis from CRISIL (AA-/Stable). Barriers are certification and capital, not cost: 50–60% of raw materials are imported (nickel, cobalt, molybdenum), and on the Q1 call management said all orders are now competitively bid, none on nomination. The line “only titanium alloy maker in India” appears in search summaries of company descriptions and is not verified here.

The Q1 FY27 Margin Squeeze vs the Revenue Jump — Read It Two Ways

Bull: revenue from operations rose 40.46% to ₹239.49 Cr, PAT +27.42% to ₹16.31 Cr (consolidated ₹16.47 Cr vs ₹13.0 Cr), and management expects margins to normalise by Q3 as the Q1 raw-material variance (molybdenum, tungsten, nickel, cobalt; ~₹13 Cr) and the fuel and freight spike fade.
