Company Overview

What They Do

Aegis Logistics is a gas logistics company — not an LPG distributor only. It sources and sells LPG and other gases (FY26 distribution volume 754,000 MT; logistics throughput 5.152 million MT), runs an autogas retail network, and owns the LPG and liquid terminals business through Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd (AVTL, NSE: AEGISVOPAK). AVTL listed on 2 Jun 2025, is consolidated by Aegis, and the FY26 annual report calls it a subsidiary (some sources say “joint venture”; this report follows the company’s wording). Aegis holds 44.71% of AVTL after the IPO diluted it from 50.10%; Vopak India BV holds 42.23%.

FY26 consolidated sales were ₹8,333 Cr, operating profit ₹1,452 Cr (Screener) and net profit ₹1,107 Cr. Q1 FY27 was a record: sales ₹2,357 Cr (+37%), operating profit ₹714 Cr (30% margin), gas-division EBITDA ₹591 Cr (+296%) on a distribution margin of ~₹7,000/MT versus ~₹4,000/MT through FY25. Five terminal and storage regions, six ports (Mumbai, Kandla, Pipavav, Mangalore, Haldia, Kochi).

Key Competitive Advantage: The terminal footprint — six ports, the only private operator with LPG import terminals at four locations (per source), protected by port leases and environmental/explosives licences — sitting beside a trading arm with a large LPG import share (company-claimed ~20% of India’s LPG imports; unverified). Caveat: India Ratings notes no long-term contractual cash flows in the gas and liquid segments overall, so terminal economics rest mainly on high utilisation, with specific contracted exceptions (see §02).
FY26 Segment EBIT Mix (India Ratings, 17 Sep 2026)
Gas is ~90% of revenue (lowest-margin, fixed-dollar-spread sourcing) and ~65% of FY26 EBITDA per Ind-Ra; ~73% of segment EBIT here (my arithmetic, 1,000 / 1,370) — different bases, both shown.
Earnings-Quality Rating: Where The Profit Comes From
Mix rated 3 / 5 ■■■■■

Bull: gas EBIT margin rose from 8.6% (FY24) to 26.4% (Q1 FY27); liquid logistics earns 55–62% EBIT margins and is the stable, higher-quality leg (EBIT ₹370 Cr FY26).

Bear: Q1 FY27 leaned on the lower-quality, non-contracted leg — gas EBIT ₹580 Cr in one quarter vs ₹1,000 Cr for all of FY26. The mix moved toward the volatile segment, while the capex runway (below) is aimed at the stable one. Terminalling throughput (“changeover”) was 21x in FY26 vs 38x in FY25.

What This Moat Does NOT Protect Against

Trading margin: the sourcing leg earns a fixed-dollar spread with no contracted floor, and OMCs are customers and competitors. Customer concentration: two annual-report notes do not reconcile — standalone Customer 1 = 12.29% of revenue (FY25 13.01%); the consolidated gas-segment note shows Customer 1 = 22% (FY25 10%) and Customer 2 = 3% (FY25 30%), unnamed. CARE says top-10 customers are 35–50% of income; 5 trading houses supplied 86.81% of purchases (83.95% prior). Competition: Adani Hazira (720,000 kl) and Mundra liquid terminals, IMC, the GAIL pipeline; new competing capacity not found.

Synthesis: A genuine infrastructure moat in terminals (barriers, port access, ~55-62% segment EBIT margins) attached to a trading business with no moat and a one-off-looking Q1 margin. The market currently pays for the first while earning the second. Rating agency view: India Ratings IND AA / Positive (Stable in Mar 2024, Positive since 27 Jun 2025); a separate secondary summary of CARE AA/A1+ stable (Mar 2026) is unverified and not relied on.

The Q1 FY27 Margin Surge — Read It Two Ways

Bull: record quarter — normalised consolidated EBITDA ₹727 Cr (+184%), gas EBIT ₹580 Cr (26.4% margin) and India Ratings and management both expect ~₹7,000/MT in FY27 (Ind-Ra FY27 EBITDA expectation ~₹1,800 Cr). Liquids EBITDA ₹136 Cr (~76% margin, +28%). FY26 operating cash flow was ₹2,028 Cr, helped by favourable working-capital changes (Ind-Ra).

Bear: the margin is on non-contracted sourcing volumes, and management itself says part of it is an “uncertainty margin”; sustainability is the key monitorable. One independent analyst (not a brokerage) puts Q1 economics nearer ₹12,000/MT and each ₹1,000/MT change at ~₹30 Cr EBITDA.
